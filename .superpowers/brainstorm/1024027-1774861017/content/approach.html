Which improvement approach fits your goals?

All three increase subscribers, revenue, and SEO — they differ in scope and speed of impact.

A

Targeted High-Impact Fixes (Recommended)

Fix the 5-6 specific gaps that give the biggest return right now — without touching the overall design. Fast to ship, low risk.

  • Add og:image — social share cards currently show no image (kills Facebook/Twitter CTR)
  • Add social proof to homepage hero (e.g. "47,000+ incidents tracked", subscriber count)
  • Move subscribe CTA above the fold on mobile
  • Add JSON-LD structured data to all remaining pages (courts, jail, warrants)
  • Build Tailwind CSS instead of loading from CDN (cuts ~300KB, boosts page speed score)
  • Add og:image per-page fallback using a generated preview card
B

Conversion-Rate Overhaul

Redesign the key conversion touchpoints — homepage hero, subscribe page, advertise pages — while keeping the overall visual identity.

  • Everything in A, plus:
  • New homepage hero with a prominent email capture as the primary action
  • Redesigned advertise pages with clearer pricing, social proof, and a simpler checkout flow
  • Redesigned donate page with impact storytelling (how donations fund coverage)
  • Sticky header with subscribe CTA on all public pages
C

Full Modern Redesign

Rebuild all public templates with a modern news-site layout — larger type, more white space, card-based feeds, persistent sidebar CTAs.

  • Everything in A + B, plus:
  • Redesigned feed layout with article card thumbnails
  • Persistent right-rail with subscribe + ad units on all content pages
  • Dedicated county hub pages with stats, recent incidents, and subscribe CTA
  • Author/about page to build editorial trust
  • Significant template work across 100+ files
