# Facebook / Instagram ad copy variants for Montana Blotter lawyer acquisition

All variants are built for Montana-target audiences. Lead with the hook, make the
promise concrete, put the URL in the CTA. Each variant includes primary text,
headline, description (optional), and CTA button text.

---

## Variant A — Pro subscription: discipline monitoring + records access
Audience: Montana licensed attorneys (criminal defense, DUI, family law, solo + small firms)
Objective: conversions to /pro/attorneys → Pro checkout

Primary text:
Montana Blotter ingests the Office of Disciplinary Counsel's monthly public discipline
list automatically — and indexes jail bookings, court records, and daily blotters from
all 56 counties. A Pro subscription ($19.99/mo) adds the full searchable archive, name
and keyword watchlists, statewide alerts, and CSV/PDF exports. Add your name to a
watchlist and we'll flag any new discipline entry the moment it publishes.
URL: montanablotter.com/pro/attorneys

Headline:
Monitor your name on Montana's discipline list.

Description:
Full archive, statewide alerts, CSV/PDF exports.

CTA button:
Learn More  (or Subscribe / Sign Up if the pixel is warm)

## Variant B — Directory sponsorship: Silver / Gold placement

When someone gets booked in your county, their family searches for a lawyer within the
hour. Montana Blotter ranks #1 in Google for "county jail roster" and "arrests today"
across the top Montana counties — and our warrant detail and county arrest pages already
showcase local defense attorneys. Silver Featured ($99/mo) pins your firm above the free

and custom callout.

## Usage notes

- Run A and B as separate ad sets against Montana geo + attorney-interest audiences
  (job titles, industry, pages liked) so you can compare discipline-monitoring vs
  client-intent traffic angles.
- Run C as a broad-reach awareness ad with no hard offer — retarget page visitors with
  A or B.
- The Pro landing page lives at /pro/attorneys. The sponsorship landing page lives at
  /advertise/attorney-sponsorship. Both now have Stripe checkout wired in.
- Track the downstream conversion: free directory signups → sponsored tier upgrades,
  Pro subscribers → ODC watchlist activations.
