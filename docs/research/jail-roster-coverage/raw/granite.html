GRANITE COUNTY PUBLIC MAP

Over the last 25+ years, Granite County has developed and maintained an Enhanced 9-1-1 System for the County, including Global Positioning System (GPS) and Geographic Information System (GIS) Mapping and Addressing. The intent of the overall E-911 system is to ensure that persons dialing 9-1-1 within the County are routed to the Granite County Sheriff’s Department, where their name, address, phone number, and map location will automatically display on screen to assist dispatchers in facilitating emergency response. 

Recognizing that the GIS data is beneficial to everyone, not just County employees, the County Commissioners worked through the County’s E-911 & GPS/GIS Consultant, Mapping and Planning Specialists, Inc. (MaPS, Inc.), who partnered with CivicLens, a GIS technology expert, to develop a PUBLIC GIS VIEWER. The Public GIS Viewer can be used by the general public, Title Companies, Real Estate agents, delivery services, the Post Office, insurance agents, etc., to assist verification of mapping and address information (e.g., what is my address, what school district are we in, which Commissioner serves my district, etc.).
