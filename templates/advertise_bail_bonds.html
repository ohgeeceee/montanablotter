{# --------------------------------------------------------------- #} {# FAQ #} {# --------------------------------------------------------------- #}

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How fast will my placement go live?

Most placements are live within 1–3 business days after contract signature, subject to creative review and county inventory.

Do you require a state license number?

Yes. All bail agency placements must provide a valid Montana state license number before launch.

What is "county exclusivity"?

A county sponsorship is limited to one agency per county. If your preferred county is already taken, you can join the waitlist or pick a different placement.

Can I preview my ad before launch?

Yes. The live ad simulator previews your banner or sidebar unit in the actual /bail-bonds reading context before you commit.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel any time from the control panel. No contracts, no fees. Your placement comes down at the end of the current billing period.

What is included in the monthly report?

Impression, click, call, and lead counts. The Gold Bond Bundle also includes cost-per-lead and a counties-covered breakdown.
