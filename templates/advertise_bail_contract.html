1. Inventory and Placement

Montana Blotter sells limited ad inventory for bail bond providers, including header placements, sidebar placements, county exclusives, and bundled packages. Placement availability is not guaranteed until payment is received and county inventory is confirmed.

2. Creative Review and Compliance

All logos, headlines, body copy, call-to-action language, and destination URLs are subject to review before launch. Montana Blotter may reject, pause, or require revisions for creative that is misleading, unlicensed, non-compliant, or inappropriate for public safety reporting context.

3. Billing and Renewal

Advertising subscriptions renew automatically on the selected monthly or annual cycle unless canceled. Taxes, platform processing fees, and custom production work are not included unless expressly stated in writing.

4. County Exclusives and Bundles

Exclusive county sponsorships and bundle-based county slots are assigned based on current inventory. If a requested county becomes unavailable before launch, Montana Blotter may offer a substitute county, delayed launch timing, account credit, or cancellation before launch.

5. Cancellation and Changes

Campaign changes may be requested through the advertiser onboarding flow or control panel. Cancellation requests should be submitted before the next billing cycle to avoid renewal charges. Mid-cycle cancellations do not automatically create prorated refunds unless Montana Blotter approves an exception in writing.

6. Performance and Availability

Traffic, impressions, clicks, calls, and lead volume can vary by county, news cycle, seasonality, and inventory mix. Montana Blotter does not guarantee any specific booking volume, conversion rate, or legal outcome from advertising.
