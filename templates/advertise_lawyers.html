{# --------------------------------------------------------------- #} {# FAQ #} {# --------------------------------------------------------------- #}

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How fast will my listing go live?

Immediately after Stripe checkout confirms. The directory updates in real time — you can refresh /lawyers and see your card.

Do you require a Montana State Bar number?

Yes. All paid placements must provide a valid Montana State Bar number. We may verify it before activating your listing.

What does Gold priority lead routing mean?

When a consumer submits the intake form on /lawyers, Gold advertisers in that county get the lead first. Silver and Bronze are notified only if the Gold advertiser doesn't claim it within a short window.

What if a county is full?

Caps are 1 Gold / 2 Silver / 2 Bronze per county. If your county is full, you can join a waitlist or pick a different county. We will reach out when a slot opens.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel any time from your control panel. No contracts, no fees. Your listing comes down at the end of the current billing period.

Do I have to provide a logo?

Logos are required for Silver and Gold, optional for Bronze. We accept JPG, PNG, and WebP up to 2 MB.
