What this means for {{ firm.name }}

This is the exact format and metric set every {{ report.package.name }} advertiser receives at the start of each month. For {{ firm.name }} on the {{ firm.county_display }} County page, that means:

  • Your firm's card appears at the top of the {{ firm.county_display }} County section on /lawyers.
  • Tap-to-call and tap-to-text are enabled on your card — mobile visitors can reach the {{ firm.name }} office with one tap.
  • Consumer intake leads from the {{ firm.county_display }} County page are emailed to you first (priority lead routing); Silver and Bronze firms in the county are notified only if the lead is not claimed within the short window.
  • Every new booking in {{ firm.county_display }} County can trigger a real-time arrest-alert email to your firm within minutes of ingestion (Gold-tier firms are contacted first).
  • At the start of each month you get this report and a control-panel link where you can update your photo, tagline, description, and CTA without a login.
