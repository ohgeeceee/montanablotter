What's on Montana Blotter

The platform aggregates seven categories of public record from all 56 Montana counties. Each record links back to the original government source so you can verify it.

🚔 Police Blotters

Daily incident reports, calls for service, and dispatch logs from city police and sheriff's offices.

🔒 Jail Rosters

Current inmate lists and new bookings from participating county detention centers.

⚖️ Court Activity

Case filings, hearing schedules, and dispositions from Montana district courts.

📋 Warrants & Wanted

Active warrant lists from sheriff's offices, sorted by county, with subscribe-by-county notifications.

👤 Missing Persons

Public alerts and law-enforcement missing-person bulletins from across the state.

🏛️ Public Meetings

City council, county commission, and school board agendas posted at agendas.montanablotter.com.

📄 Other Records

Code violations, license sanctions, sex-offender registry updates, government spending, and public salaries.

✏️ Editorial Blog

Daily summaries, explainers, and analysis that translate complex filings into plain English.

Searching records

The quickest way to find something is the search bar at the top of every page. You can search by name, case number, charge, agency, or location — it covers blotters, bookings, court cases, and warrants simultaneously.

Free tier includes basic search and filters for the last 7 days of records. Blotter Plus unlocks 12 months of searchable history, advanced filters (date range, incident type, charge category), and up to 10 saved searches. Blotter Pro adds the full archive and unlimited saved searches.

Browse records by county on the counties directory or drill into a specific day's blotter via the county-day view.

Alerts & monitoring

Montana Blotter can push new records to you as they're published — by email, push notification, or SMS.

County alerts

Get notified whenever a new daily blotter or booking roster is published for a specific county. Free users get 1 county, Plus users get up to 5 counties, and Pro users get statewide alerts.

Name & keyword watchlists

Set up watchlists for names, case numbers, charges, or keywords. When a matching record lands on the site, you're notified. Free tier: not available. Plus: 5 watchlists. Pro: unlimited.

Warrant notifications

Subscribe to individual warrant lists by county at /wanted. Paying subscribers get same-day alerts when new warrants are posted. Warrants are behind a separate add-on access gate for Blotter Plus+ subscribers.

Case tracking

Follow a court case and receive status-change notifications when a hearing is scheduled, a disposition is filed, or a docket entry appears. Preview: up to 5 cases (Plus). Unlimited case tracking (Pro).

The daily digest

Each morning, Montana Blotter sends a curated email digest with the previous day's new records, notable incidents, and editorial highlights. Free users get a basic digest for one county. Plus and Pro users get the full digest customized to their selected counties, with optional daily, weekly, or weekend-only delivery.

Sign up at /subscribe — no account required for the free digest.

Exports & data downloads

Need records offline or in a spreadsheet?

  • Plus — limited PDF exports of search results and case summaries.
  • Pro — full CSV and PDF exports, historical trend data, and county/agency/category visualizations.
  • API access — Pro users can request an API key for programmatic access to structured record data. Contact records@montanablotter.com.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to browse?

No. The public site is free to read without an account. You only need an account to set up alerts, save searches, or subscribe to a paid plan.

How fresh is the data?

It depends on the source county. Some publish daily PDFs that hit our ingest pipeline within minutes. Others publish weekly or sporadically. Each record shows when it was uploaded and links to the original source. The coverage page shows last-refresh time per county.

Can I download records for offline use?

Blotter Plus includes PDF exports of search results. Blotter Pro adds full CSV exports suitable for spreadsheets and analysis tools.

Is the source code available?

Yes — the project is open source. The repository includes the full Flask application, scrapers, parsers, and deployment configuration. See the GitHub repository.

Can I request a county be added?

Absolutely. Email records@montanablotter.com with the county name and any source URLs you know about. We prioritize counties with available online blotters.

Does the mobile app do the same thing?

Yes — the React Native mobile app mirrors the full public site experience, including search, browse, alerts, and account management. Available for iOS and Android via the app stores.

What about privacy?

We automatically redact victim names in domestic violence and sexual assault cases. All records are public record already — we gather them from official government sources rather than creating new data. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How can I support the project?

Subscribe to a paid plan, donate via the supporter page, or spread the word. Sponsorship inquiries go to records@montanablotter.com.
