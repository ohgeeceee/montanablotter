Blotter Records Pending

The following 49 counties have not yet submitted public activity reports to Montana Blotter. Police blotters and daily activity logs are public records under Montana law — any resident has the right to request them from their local sheriff's office.

If you live in one of these counties, consider calling your sheriff's office and asking them to publish or share their blotter. The more transparency requested by residents, the more likely agencies are to respond.

What to say when you call:

"Hi, I'm a resident of [County] County. I'd like to ask whether your office publishes a daily or weekly police blotter or activity report, and if so, how I can receive it. These are public records and I'd love to stay informed about public safety in our community."
