Help

Frequently asked questions

How fast will I hear back?

Most intake submissions are routed immediately to active advertisers and reviewed throughout the day. Gold-tier attorneys respond first.

Do I have to pay to submit a request?

No. Submitting the intake form is free. The attorneys pay for the placement; you don't pay to send the request.

Are listings vetted?

All paid advertisers must provide a Montana State Bar number. Montana Blotter reserves the right to reject listings that don't meet basic standards.

Does Montana Blotter give legal advice?

No. The directory is a starting point, not a recommendation. The Montana State Bar lawyer referral service is another resource.
