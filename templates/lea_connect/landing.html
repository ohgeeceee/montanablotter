Platform

Built for Law Enforcement

Every feature was designed from the ground up around the workflows, security requirements, and statutory obligations of Montana law enforcement agencies.

Real-Time Publishing

Submit an incident and it appears on montanablotter.com in under 10 minutes. No more 3–7 day scraping delays.

Review & Approval Workflow

Draft, review, approve, or reject submissions through your agency dashboard. Two-person control ensures nothing goes live without authorization.

Batch Upload & CSV Import

Upload dozens of incidents at once from your RMS/CAD export. Preview every record before publishing. Full validation built in.

Role-Based Access Control

Granular permissions for Agency Admin, Records Officer, and PIO. Each role sees exactly what it needs and nothing more.

REST API Integration

Connect your existing RMS via secure token-based API. Rate-limited, logged, and CJIS-compliant out of the box.

Immutable Audit Trail

Every action — submission, approval, rejection, edit — is logged with timestamp, user, IP, and previous state. No deletions.

Workflow

Three Steps to Publication

From your dashboard to the public record in minutes. No IT setup required.

1

Submit

An officer or records staff submits an incident through the dashboard or API. It saves as a secure draft, visible only to your agency.

2

Review & Approve

A supervisor or PIO reviews the submission. PII is automatically flagged. The record is approved or returned with revision notes.

3

Publish

Approved records are normalized, PII-redacted, and published to montanablotter.com within minutes. Your agency maintains full control.

Security & Compliance

CJIS-Compliant by Design

LEA Connect was architected from day one to meet the Criminal Justice Information Services security standards required by Montana law enforcement.

CJIS Security Policy

Access control, audit logging, transmission security, and personnel screening enforced at every layer.

Agency Verification

Every agency is verified via ORI number matching and government-issued email domain (.gov, .mt.us) before gaining access.

Immutable Audit Log

All actions logged with cryptographic integrity. Logs cannot be altered or deleted — meeting evidentiary standards for public records.
