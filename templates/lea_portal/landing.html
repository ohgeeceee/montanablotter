Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the LEA Portal.

Is this really free for law enforcement agencies?

Yes. The LEA Portal is completely free for all Montana law enforcement agencies. There are no setup fees, monthly charges, or per-incident costs. Montana Blotter is an open-source public records platform — this is part of our mission to increase government transparency.

How long does it take for a submission to go live?

Once an incident is submitted and approved by a supervisor/PIO, it is typically published to the public site within 5–10 minutes. The normalization and PII audit run automatically every 5 minutes.

Can I edit or remove a published incident?

Published incidents cannot be deleted (to maintain audit integrity), but they can be updated by submitting a correction. The audit trail preserves the original and the correction. Contact the admin team if you need to remove something urgently.

What if my county doesn't have an ORI number?

An ORI number is preferred but not required for registration. We can verify your agency through government email domain (.gov, .mt.us) or by phone. Fill out the registration form and we'll work it out.

Can I submit jail rosters in addition to blotters?

Yes. The portal supports both police blotter incidents and jail roster snapshots. Rosters sync every 4 hours and are deduplicated automatically. Enable roster publishing in your agency settings.

How does PII redaction work?

Before publication, the system automatically scans narratives for potential PII (names, DOBs, SSNs, addresses). Flagged content is highlighted for review. Sensitive records (juveniles, domestic violence victims) require explicit admin approval before going public.

Can our IT department integrate via API?

Absolutely. We offer a REST API with token-based authentication. Endpoints include incident submission, batch upload, roster sync, and audit log retrieval. Rate limited at 1,000 requests per hour per agency. See the API docs once logged in.

🛡

CJIS-Compliant Security

The LEA Portal was built with law enforcement security requirements from day one. Every audit log entry is immutable — no edits, no deletions. Role-based access is enforced at the database level. Agency verification requires a government email domain (.gov, .mt.us) and ORI number validation.
