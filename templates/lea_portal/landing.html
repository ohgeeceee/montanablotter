How It Works

Three steps to go from submission to publication.

1

Submit

An officer submits an incident through the dashboard or API. It saves as a draft.

2

Review

A supervisor or PIO reviews and approves the submission. PII is automatically flagged.

3

Publish

Approved incidents are normalized and published to the public site within minutes.

🛡

CJIS-Compliant Security

The LEA Portal was built with law enforcement security requirements from day one. Every audit log entry is immutable — no edits, no deletions. Role-based access control is enforced at the database level. Agency verification requires a government email domain (.gov, .mt.us) and ORI number validation.
