Disclaimer

Not legal advice.

This course summarizes published Montana law. Statutes change. Verify every citation against the current text at leg.mt.gov/bills/mca and consult a licensed Montana attorney for your specific situation.

Editorial standards

Every claim in this course links to a primary Montana Code section on leg.mt.gov. Where a chapter refers to a real case, we cite the public court record. We do not invent scenarios, fictional stops, or made-up dispositions.

The site's Sourcing Standards and Corrections Policy control this course. If you spot a citation that has drifted from the current statute, email records@montanablotter.com with subject line Course Citation Update.

What this course is not

  • Not legal advice. If you are facing an actual legal problem, talk to a licensed Montana attorney. The Montana State Bar has a lawyer referral service.
  • Not a substitute for the MCA. The MCA is free and authoritative. This course paraphrases it.
  • Not a guarantee that citations will stay current. The Montana Legislature meets every two years. Statutes change. Email records@montanablotter.com if you find one that has drifted.

How this course is maintained

Montana Blotter is a small team in Great Falls. This course is updated annually after each regular session of the Montana Legislature, and on an ad-hoc basis when we receive a citation correction. Each chapter carries a last-reviewed date. The site's Sourcing Standards page explains our editorial process; the Corrections Policy explains how to report something that is wrong.

How to help

  • Report a citation drift. If a section number has changed or a statute has been amended, email us with the chapter number, the section heading, and the URL we linked.
  • Tell us what is missing. If a chapter glosses over the part you actually need, tell us.
  • Share the course. It is free and it is intended to be read. The link is montanablotter.com/learn.
  • Fund the work. Montana Blotter is supported by reader donations and a small handful of civic-tech sponsors. See the transparency page.
