About this list

The Montana Office of Disciplinary Counsel publishes a public list of attorneys currently or previously licensed in Montana who have been publicly disciplined.

The list is compiled from public orders of the Montana Supreme Court or Commission on Practice. Private admonitions are not public record and are excluded.
