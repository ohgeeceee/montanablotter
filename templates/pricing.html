Frequently Asked Questions

What happens when I hit my preview limit?

You'll see a blurred summary of the incident or booking with an option to upgrade. List views, search, and the crime map remain fully accessible.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancel from your account page or by contacting support. You'll keep access until the end of your billing period.

Is my payment secure?

All payments are processed securely through Stripe. We never store your card details.
