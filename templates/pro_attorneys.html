What attorneys ask before subscribing.

Is the ODC discipline list included?

Yes — the searchable discipline page at /attorney-discipline is freely accessible to everyone. Pro adds the underlying full dataset, the ability to export it, and the option to monitor your own name or opponents through watchlists and alerts.

Can I sponsor my firm and subscribe Pro?

Yes. The sponsorship tiers (Silver $99/mo, Gold $199/mo) are separate products for directory placement. Pro ($19.99/mo) is the data subscription. Many firms do both — sponsor their county listing and subscribe Pro for the records access.

Is this a substitute for a bar-verification check?

No. Montana Blotter publishes public records. You are responsible for verifying your own and opposing counsel's bar status directly with the Montana State Bar. The ODC list is a public resource — use it as one signal among several.
