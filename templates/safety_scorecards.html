Neighborhood Safety Scorecards

Synthesized incident metrics by area. Lower incident volume and severity yield higher safety scores.

Methodology

Scores are computed from total incidents, incident diversity, and average severity over a 30-day window. Percentiles compare against other Montana areas. Scores range 0–100 where 100 indicates the safest relative conditions.
