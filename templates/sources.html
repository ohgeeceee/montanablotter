Known gaps

We're publishing this list so the limits of our data are visible. If a source is on the blocked list, the blotter, booking, warrant, and court pages reflect that gap — they don't quietly fall back to empty.

Some county sheriff feeds require manual UI login

Several Montana county sheriff offices use portals that reject automated connections from datacenter IPs. Roster and booking data from those counties is therefore not flowing in automatically. We're working with each agency's public-records contact on a documented path. In the meantime, those counties are listed as blocked or stale on this page.
