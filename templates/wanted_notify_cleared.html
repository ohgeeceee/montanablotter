Notification Signup

Tell us which warrant to watch.

Fields marked with * are required. The more specific you can be, the lower the chance of a false match. We match on warrant ID first; if you don't have it, we match on name plus date of birth.

How It Works

What happens after you submit.

1. We store the request

Your submission is stored privately. The only fields we keep are the ones you provided and your IP address (for spam prevention). It is not indexed or shown to anyone.

2. We watch for the status change

When a warrant moves from active to cleared or resolved, our pipeline checks for matching requests — by warrant ID first, then by name plus date of birth.

3. We email once

You receive a single notification. There are no follow-ups, no newsletters, and no marketing. After the email goes out, the request is marked as notified and not contacted again.

4. Verify directly

The notification points you to the original Montana source. We are an aggregator, not a court; the official record is what controls.

Need help fast?

This is not legal advice.

If you have an active warrant, the right next step is to consult a licensed Montana defense attorney who can appear on your behalf and, in many cases, clear the warrant without a custody event. Montana Blotter does not provide legal advice.

Privacy

We do not sell or share signup data.

The email address is used only for the single notification. After delivery, it remains in our database solely so we can audit duplicate or fraudulent requests; it is not used for marketing.
